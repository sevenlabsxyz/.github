# SEVEN LABS

> A small family lab, made on Earth.

We build strange, useful things for the open internet.
Sometimes it is software. Sometimes it is clothing. Sometimes it is a hackathon in a city we love.

We like freedom technology, open source, Bitcoin, good design, human-scale companies, and ideas that are a little too weird to survive a committee.

## Things we make

- **[The Original](https://theoriginal.art)** — wearable art, independent clothing, small runs, no mass-market vibes.
- **[Evento](https://evento.so)** — open-source events, tickets, and peer-to-peer Bitcoin payments. No middlemen, no weird platform tax energy.
- **[Hack4Freedom](https://hack4freedom.com)** — hackathons and mentorship across the Global South, helping builders learn freedom tech by building it.
- **[Lightning Address](https://lightningaddress.com)** — an open protocol we created for human-readable Lightning payments. Like email, but for your Bitcoin.
- **[NoBadNews](https://nobadnews.app)** — seven genuinely good things that happened yesterday. Coming soon.

## How we work

We are opinionated, but not precious.

We prefer:

- open protocols over closed gardens
- peer-to-peer over platform choke points
- useful over impressive
- beautiful over merely functional
- shipping over performing productivity
- small teams over big theater
- weird ideas over safe decks

If it helps people do more with less, we are probably interested.

## Open source

A lot of what we make lives in public, breaks in public, and gets better in public. That's this GitHub page you're in!

## Work with the lab

We occasionally work with startups, brands, founders, and funds, on Bitcoin and Lightning strategy, open-source architecture, technical diligence, founder mentorship, branding, and custom company clothing.

If it is interesting, we should probably talk.
